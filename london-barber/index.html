About London Barber

A Cut Above, Right on Edgware Road

London Barber is the neighbourhood's go-to barbershop for precision grooming — a two-minute walk from Paddington and Marble Arch, and the shop regulars trust for fades that actually hold their line.

We blend traditional barbering craft — hot towel shaves, cut-throat shaves, proper straight-razor technique — with sharp, modern precision styling. Every visit starts with a real conversation, not a rushed clipper guard. We look at your face shape, your beard growth, your hair type, and suggest what will actually work, not just what was asked for.
